Feeling sick is very common in early pregnancy. It is not only in the morning. It usually settles by 16 to 20 SA.

## What can help

- Rest. Tiredness makes nausea worse.
- Eat a dry biscuit or a piece of toast before getting out of bed.
- Eat small, plain meals often. Do not stay a long time without eating.
- Cold food often smells less than hot food.
- Skip spicy, fried and fatty food.
- Cut down on tea, coffee and other drinks with caffeine.
- Sip water little by little, especially after vomiting.
- Get up slowly, open a window and wear loose clothes.
- Avoid the smells or foods that set it off.
- Ginger may calm a mild upset stomach. Ask a pharmacist before taking ginger supplements.

Do not take medicine for the sickness on your own. If it does not get better, your doctor or midwife can help.
