Sore, heavy or swollen breasts are one of the early signs of pregnancy. They often start from 6 SA and can last into the weeks that follow. Your breasts also grow bigger, and the skin around the nipples can get darker. This darkening fades after the birth.

## What can help

- Know that it is normal. It is caused by pregnancy hormones.
- Ask your midwife or doctor for advice on bras and comfort if the soreness bothers you.
