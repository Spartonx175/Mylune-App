Feeling very tired is one of the first signs of pregnancy. Your body is working hard, and you have more blood to pump. It is not a sign that something is wrong.

## What can help

- Rest with your feet up when you can.
- Accept help with chores.
- Eat regular, healthy meals.
- Go for a short walk. Even gentle exercise can help.
- Use pillows to find a comfortable position to sleep.
- Try to relax before bed.
- Avoid tea, coffee and cola in the evening.
- Try to avoid long naps in the afternoon.
- Strange dreams are common and normal.
