Slow bowels are common in pregnancy. They can also cause mild tummy pain, which is usually harmless.

## What can help

- Eat foods rich in fibre: wholemeal bread, fruit, vegetables and pulses.
- Drink plenty of water.
- Move regularly. A daily walk helps.
- If you take iron, ask your doctor if you can manage without it or take a different kind. Do not stop it by yourself.
