Mood swings are common in early pregnancy. Hormones change fast, and a pregnancy is also a big life change. Feeling happy, worried and tearful in the same day can be normal.

Depression and anxiety can happen in pregnancy too. They are common, and they can be treated.

## What can help

- Talk to someone you trust, such as your partner, a friend or your midwife.
- Eat balanced meals.
- Stay active. A walk helps your mood and your sleep.
- Take time for things you enjoy.
- Go to the preparation classes. You meet other parents.
- Be honest with your midwife or doctor about how you feel.
