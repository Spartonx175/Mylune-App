At 12 SA (SG 10), your baby is about the size of a plum. The placenta is now doing the work of feeding your baby, and the heartbeat can be seen on ultrasound.

For many women, sickness starts to ease around now. You may be hungrier too. There is no need to eat for two, only to eat well.

This is the time to finish the first steps. The first ultrasound, the first consultation and the declaration of pregnancy should all be done or planned.
