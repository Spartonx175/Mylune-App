At 7 SA (SG 5), your baby has grown to about the size of a blueberry. The brain is developing fast, and the face is slowly taking shape.

Your body now makes more blood, which is one reason you may feel thirsty and tired. Sickness and mood changes are still common this week.

It is a good week to sort out the paperwork. The declaration of pregnancy opens your maternity cover, and your doctor or midwife can do it online.
