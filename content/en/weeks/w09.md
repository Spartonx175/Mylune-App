  - Your baby is about 22 mm long, roughly the size of an olive.
  - The face is easier to recognise, with eyelids, a tiny mouth and a tongue.
  - Hands and feet are forming, but there are no fingers or toes yet.
  - The heart, brain, lungs, kidneys and gut are developing.
mother:
  - You may feel very tired and sick.
  - Pregnancy hormones are high, so your emotions may feel strong.
  - Your breasts may feel bigger and your waist may feel thicker.
todo:
  - Check the date of your first ultrasound. It takes place between 11 SA and 13 SA plus 6 days.
  - Make sure your first prenatal consultation is booked before the end of the 3rd month.
  - Check that your declaration of pregnancy has been sent.
  - Know that a free dental check-up is available from the 4th month of pregnancy.
tip_of_day:
  - Eat little and often to keep your energy steady.
  - A well-cooked egg is an easy snack. Avoid raw or runny eggs.
  - Accept help from people around you.
  - Strong feelings are normal. Talk to someone you trust.
  - Rest in the afternoon if you can.
  - Raw carrots or cooked fruit make easy snacks. Wash raw vegetables well.
  - Look at the weather and plan a short walk.

At 9 SA (SG 7), your baby is about the size of an olive. The face is taking shape. Hands and feet have started to form too.

Your hormones are high this week. This can make you feel more emotional, as well as tired and sick. Try to rest, and accept help.

The next weeks bring your first ultrasound. Check the date, and the other appointments that go with it.
