todo:
  - Tell a doctor or midwife about your positive test, if you have not yet.
  - Book your first prenatal consultation. It should happen before the end of the 3rd month.
  - Ask your doctor or midwife about a folic acid (vitamin B9) supplement. It is advised up to 12 weeks.
  - Stop smoking and stop alcohol completely. Ask for help if this is hard.
tip_of_day:
  - Eat small meals often. An empty stomach can make nausea worse.
  - A dry biscuit or toast before getting out of bed can help in the morning.
  - Rest when you can. Feeling tired is normal this week.
  - Sip water through the day, in small amounts.
  - Cold food often smells less strong than hot food.
  - Take a short walk outside. Gentle movement can help your tiredness.
  - Write down any questions for your first consultation.

You are at 6 SA, which is about 4 weeks since conception (SG 4). Your baby is still very small, but a lot is already happening. The heart has started to beat and the first buds of the arms and legs are showing.

You may not look pregnant, but your body is working hard. Feeling sick, tired or emotional is very common now. It is usually a sign that your hormones are doing their job.

This week, the main thing is to get your follow-up started. Tell your doctor or midwife, and book the first consultation.
