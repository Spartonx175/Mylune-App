todo:
  - Make sure your first prenatal consultation is booked. It should happen before the end of the 3rd month.
  - Ask your doctor or midwife about the first ultrasound. It takes place between 11 SA and 13 SA plus 6 days.
  - Ask about the blood tests, including toxoplasmosis immunity.
  - Ask about the early prenatal interview (entretien prénatal précoce). It is advised from the 4th month, but you can start in the first trimester.
tip_of_day:
  - Drink more during the day and a little less in the evening. This can help your sleep.

  - Take a few quiet minutes to breathe slowly.
  - Wash fruit and vegetables well. This is important for toxoplasmosis.
  - Choose a calm activity you enjoy this evening.
  - Wear a comfortable bra if your breasts feel sore.
  - Note the date of your first ultrasound in your calendar.

At 8 SA (SG 6), your baby is about the size of a raspberry. The head is starting to straighten, and the arms are getting longer. From around now, your baby is called a foetus.

The placenta is also growing. It will soon feed your baby. You may feel tired, bloated or sick, and you may need the toilet more often.

Look ahead this week. The first ultrasound and the first blood tests are coming up in the next weeks.
