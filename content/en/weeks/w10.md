At 10 SA (SG 8), your baby is about 3 cm long. The movements have started, but they are far too small for you to feel.

Pregnancy hormones slow your gut down. This can cause bloating, wind and heartburn. Small meals and slow eating usually help.

Get your appointments in order this week. The first ultrasound is due very soon.
